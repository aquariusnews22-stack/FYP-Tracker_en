Your module practice

Regulation Bridge · between Prepare and Perform

Reset Your State. Return to Performance.

You do not need to feel completely calm. Choose a tool that helps you return to the next useful action.

In the kick-off, try one simple reset. Weeks 1–2: practise Stress Reset in training. After GO × PREPARE, connect your trained anchor. Week 4: explore the full Regulation Bridge. Practise a new tool in training before relying on it in competition.
