Core Toolkit

These tools work across every phase of the programme. Choose the one that fits the moment — you do not need to use all of them every time.

When each one is introduced:

Kick-off — a first Regulation reset, and the Learning Engine, available from day one.
Week 2 — Ask Better Questions and Assumption Check, alongside the belief work in Free × Prepare.
Week 4 — the full Regulation Bridge.
Perform weeks — the Communication Loop (SAFE / PACE / STOP / CANT), once coach or team communication becomes relevant.
Pursue weeks — Decision Compass and Plan the Next Move.

Deep Reflection

Use this after an important performance, a difficult moment or a recurring pattern—not after every session. Complete it when you are calm enough to be curious, ideally within 24 hours. Work from facts to pattern to next action.
